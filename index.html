About us

Spear Marketing Limited

Spear Marketing Limited is a wholesale distributor of fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG) based in Chaguanas, Trinidad and Tobago.

We import household, cleaning and consumer products and distribute them to retailers and supermarkets throughout Trinidad and Tobago — from our warehouse on John Street to shops across the country.

Company facts

  • Legal name: Spear Marketing Limited
  • Incorporated in Trinidad and Tobago in 2007
  • Wholesale FMCG import and distribution
  • Based in Chaguanas
